Player 1 Keyboard Controls

Move: WASD

Attack Zero: J

Attack One: K

Attack Two: U

Attack Three: I

Player 2 Keyboard Controls

Move: Arrows

Attack Zero: Numpad 2

Attack One: Numpad 3

Attack Two: Numpad 5

Attack Three: Numpad 6

Gamepad Controls

Move: D-Pad/Left-Joystick

Attack Zero: A

Attack One: B

Attack Two: X

Attack Three: Y

Inputs (Assumed facing right)

Two-jab to roundhouse: 2, 3, 1.

Dragon initiate: Forward, down, down-forward. (Currently this only initiates an animation. This will be used as a base point for various attacks/combos.)
